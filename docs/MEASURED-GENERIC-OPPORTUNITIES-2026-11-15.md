## High-cost cases which must NOT be naively generalized

- `FlushingEtiquette` ranks **46.326 ms/s**, but its minimap `gameuiBaseMinimapMappinController::wrapper$205934` **12,140.372 ms** is dominated by its **unresolved virtual `proxy$205935`** callsite (**12,125.039 ms**, 8,586 invocations, max **174.36 ms**). The *known* FE `PinData` path is only **14.058 ms**. Source has `wrappedMethod()` followed by an optional pin tint. **Do not blame FE's tint logic, drop `wrappedMethod`, or turn this into a generic dormancy pass.** Resolve underlying proxy/native ownership and wrapper chains first. Its major script-work peaks occur near PresentMon long intervals, but that alone does not establish causation.
- `ReinforcementsSystem` geometry work: `IsPointInQuad` **1,272.698 ms**, `SortSquare` **1,053.831 ms**, active in only **5.232%** of frames, with burst spikes. Examine for structurally safe generic loop/redundant-work transformations; do not build a bespoke semantic rule from this ranking by default.
- Frequent `PlayerPuppet.OnGameAttached` (60 wrappers / 39 owners in dated source snapshot) and `OnDetach` (26 / 16) offer common **registration and lifecycle structure**, not automatically repeated hot-path CPU. These are safety/invalidation anchors for A/D, not an excuse to merge all mod callbacks.
- GRSP `RSP_WorkMap` classified measured callsite work as `FRAME_BOUND_POLLING` **12,815.070 ms**, `FRAME_BOUND_REPEAT_WORK` **11,946.300 ms**, `INNER_LOOP_EXPLOSION` **7,058.763 ms** and `HIGH_COST_CALL` **14,843.238 ms**. These are coarse evidence *labels*, not proven safe transformations or expected savings.
